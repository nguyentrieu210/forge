# Commercial Compatibility — v1.0.0

## Permitted description

**CloudForge ERPNext Business Suite RC**: a Cloudflare-native ERP release candidate with implemented transaction subsets across sales, purchasing, accounting, stock, manufacturing, assets, projects, quality, support, expenses, POS, bounded bank reconciliation, payroll accounting/grouping, subscription scheduling and provider-safe e-invoice queueing.

## Prohibited claims

- Complete ERPNext replacement or full feature parity.
- Drop-in Frappe/Python app compatibility.
- Certified accounting, payroll, tax or e-invoice compliance in any jurisdiction.
- Complete HRMS, CRM, portal, bank auto-matching, MRP/subcontracting or country localization.
- Automatic subscription billing or payment collection.
- Production readiness before exact-artifact promotion evidence passes.

## Commercially safer scope

Use only with design partners who accept the published feature matrix, staged migration, daily reconciliation, country-specific review and explicit exclusions in the contract. Treat new v0.8–v1.0 modules as RC until Workerd/Vite, pinned oracle/differential, staging, load, security, rollback and tenant-restore gates pass.

E-invoice records may be used as an internal provider queue/audit trail only after a separately certified adapter and legal acceptance process is completed for the target jurisdiction.
